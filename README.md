# Research Statement

Public-facing research statement for Yoshitaka Inoue.

The statement is written in LaTeX and centers on a research program around intervention-conditioned biological modeling:

1. transferable representations of treatment-induced change,
2. multimodal and spatial biological context for transfer, and
3. mechanism- and evidence-aware reasoning, including uncertainty and abstention.
